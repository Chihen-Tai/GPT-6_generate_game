## Separate licenses, not blanket relicensing

The root MIT LICENSE applies to Aurelia's own source code, scene definitions, shaders, tools, translations and documentation. It does not replace the licenses of third-party models, textures, animations, fonts, engine components, or modified versions of those works. Adapted CC0 artwork remains identified as CC0; the bundled font remains under OFL 1.1; Godot and its dependencies retain their individual notices.

The author credits and source records are deliberately retained even where CC0 does not require attribution. CC0 does not waive trademarks or other people's publicity/privacy rights, and it does not authorize implying an author's endorsement. Keep these notices and upstream license files with redistributed game packages. License texts, not this summary, define the applicable permissions.

## Source records and redistribution

assets/vendor/sources.json and catalog.json record source URLs, resource paths and hashes. art/vendor/downloads.json records original character/animation downloads. The licenses directory includes the CC0 legal text and original author notices; asset folders retain their individual notices. Local source-download archives and generated Blender files are not part of the Git distribution.

Aurelia's procedural scene assembly, original meshes and synthesized audio are distinct from these third-party works. Screenshots and compiled game resources can contain several separately licensed components; they are not blanket-relicensed by the root LICENSE.
